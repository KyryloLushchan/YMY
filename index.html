An intimate studio for unforgettable moments.

YMY Makeup Academy is a premier beauty destination in Da Nang, dedicated to enhancing your natural beauty for life's most precious moments. With years of expertise in bridal and event makeup, we believe every client deserves a personalised, luxurious experience.

Our studio combines professional artistry with high-quality cosmetics to create timeless, elegant looks — whether you're preparing for your wedding day, a special event, or pursuing a career in makeup artistry.
